# Eviory

**Building the infrastructure for modern digital work.**

Eviory is a software company exploring how people can plan, create, organize, and execute digital work through a more connected workspace.

We build products around a simple idea:

> **Software should help you move from thinking to execution.**

Our work spans productivity software, visual workspaces, AI-assisted workflows, and systems for digital businesses.

---

## What We're Building

### Eviory OS

Our core product ecosystem for digital work.

Eviory brings planning, creation, organization, and execution into a connected workspace designed for modern creators, founders, and teams.

### Eviory Docs

A structured workspace for documents, knowledge, and AI-assisted creation.

### Eviory Board

A visual workspace for brainstorming, planning, research, and spatial thinking.

### EvioryKit

A commercial asset ecosystem for people building and selling digital products.

---

## Our Approach

We are particularly interested in the intersection of:

* Local-first software
* AI-native workflows
* Structured data
* Visual workspaces
* Browser-native applications
* MCP and agentic interfaces
* Low-infrastructure software
* Portable and user-controlled data

We believe the next generation of software will be increasingly **local, intelligent, composable, and user-controlled**.

---

## Open Work

Most of our product development is currently private while the products are being built.

We do, however, publish ideas, experiments, and architectural research as we develop them.

### Public

**Content Execution Architecture**

An open exploration of the systems and architectural ideas behind modern content execution.

It documents our thinking around how strategy, content production, distribution, data, and AI can work together as a coherent system.

---

## Building in Public, Selectively

Not everything needs to be public from day one.

Some projects are developed privately until the product, architecture, or documentation is ready to be useful to others.

When we find something worth sharing, we publish it.

That may be:

* Architecture
* Research
* Experiments
* Open-source components
* Technical ideas
* Product concepts
* Developer tooling

---

## The Long-Term Direction

We are exploring a future where software can move fluidly between:

**Ideas → Structure → Creation → Execution → Feedback**

with AI helping operate the system while people remain responsible for the decisions.

The interface may change.

The underlying principle doesn't:

**Build software that helps people do meaningful work.**
